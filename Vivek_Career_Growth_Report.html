📷Profile Photo

Your photo gets you 21× more profile views and 36× more messages. This is the single highest-ROI change you can make.

Frame
Face fills 60–70% of frame. Shoulders-up shot. Look directly at camera.
Attire
Solid-colour shirt. Dark navy, white, or charcoal. Avoid busy patterns.
Background
Plain light grey or white. Soft blurred office/wall is also fine. No selfie backgrounds.
Expression
Natural smile. Profiles with smiling photos are rated twice as likeable.
How to take it
Phone camera, portrait mode. Ask someone to take it, or use a tripod + timer. No selfie stick. Good window light from the side.

in
LinkedIn
Primary sourcing tool for 87% of recruiters globally
🥇 Priority 1
  • Profile completion: Get to "All-Star" status. Photo + banner + headline + About + 3 experiences + 5 skills + education = All-Star. All-Star profiles get 40× more recruiter reach.
  • Open to Work: Enable for recruiters only. Add all job titles you're open to, not just one. Mumbai + Thane + Navi Mumbai + Remote.

  • Recommendations: Ask one classmate from Net Tech India or one colleague to write you a short recommendation. Even one makes a difference. Profiles with recommendations show up higher in searches.
N
Naukri.com
India's largest job portal — #1 for corporate hiring
🥇 Priority 1
  • Resume freshness is everything. Upload a new PDF every 3–5 days. Naukri's algorithm ranks recently-updated profiles higher in ResDeX (recruiter search). Even a minor edit triggers a refresh.
  • Headline (80 chars): Java Developer | Spring Boot | REST APIs | MySQL | 1 yr ERP Backend
  • Key skills: Add 15–20 skills exactly matching JD language. Java, Spring Boot, Hibernate, REST API, MySQL, Microservices, Hibernate ORM, JPA, Git, Postman, Backend Development, ERP, API Integration.
  • Notice period: Set to your actual notice period or shorter — shorter notice period profiles rank higher in recruiter dashboards. If you're unsure, list 30 days.
  • Expected CTC: Research the range first (see below). Don't under-price yourself — set it at market rate.
  • Preferred locations: Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Pune, Remote. Don't select everywhere — it signals desperation and reduces targeted matches.
  • Apply manually, not auto-apply. Naukri's auto-apply has very low conversion. Manual, targeted applications to relevant JDs perform 3–5× better.
Salary benchmark for your level (Mumbai, Java, 1 yr): ₹4.5L – ₹7.5L depending on company tier. Service companies (TCS, Infosys): ₹4–5.5L. Mid-size product: ₹5.5–7.5L. Startup/fintech: ₹6–9L. Set your expected CTC at ₹6–7.5L and current CTC accurately.

Growing on LinkedIn

LinkedIn's 2025–26 algorithm change means activity now directly affects how often your profile appears in passive recruiter searches. You don't need to go viral — you need to be consistently visible.

Content Strategy — What to Post
Post Type Frequency Example for You
Technical lesson 1× / week "One thing I learned building an ERP backend: always index your foreign keys. Here's why it cut our query time in half."
Project showcase 1× / month Show Bill-Hive's PWA capabilities. Screen recording + short explanation of the technical challenge you solved.
Career story 1× / month "BMS grad → Java developer in 2 years. Here's the honest story." — Non-IT background posts do very well with Indian developer audiences.
Opinion / take 2× / month "Why I think every junior developer should work on a product, not just a service. What 1 year at a product company taught me."
Post Format That Gets Reach
Hook (line 1 — make them click "see more"): I built two backend products in one year. Almost entirely alone. Body (short paragraphs, 1-2 lines each): When I joined LNV Digital Systems, they wanted to shift from service to product. I was the backend guy. No playbook. No team. Just Java, Spring Boot, and a lot of Stack Overflow. Here's what I learned building ERP systems from scratch: → Design your DB schema before writing a single line of service code → REST contracts matter more than you think — your frontend team is your client → Write for the person who maintains this 6 months from now (often you) → Senior guidance is gold — ask dumb questions early, not late Call to action: What's one thing you wish you knew before your first backend project?
Format rules: Short lines. White space. No walls of text. Posts with questions at the end get 2–3× more comments. Comments = reach.

90-Day Action Plan

Concrete, sequenced. Do things in order — the early weeks compound into the later ones.

Week 1
Foundation — Profile & Assets
  • Take a professional headshot (follow photo guide above)
  • Update LinkedIn: new photo, headline, About section, Experience bullets, Skills (use this guide)
  • Create LinkedIn banner on Canva (1584×396px, dark navy, stack listed)
  • Enable Open to Work (recruiters only) + Creator Mode
  • Update Naukri profile fully — all fields, re-upload CV
  • Set up GitHub README for your profile
Week 2
Presence — First Posts & Connections
  • Write and publish your Origin Story post (BMS → Java Developer)
  • Connect with 50+ people: Net Tech India classmates, Mumbai Java developers, recruiters at target companies
  • Create profiles on Wellfound, Instahyre, CutShort
  • Start applying: 5–8 targeted applications per week (Naukri + Wellfound)
  • Re-upload Naukri CV (do this every 3–5 days from now on)
Weeks 3–4
Momentum — Technical Content + Applications
  • Publish Technical Win post (building products as solo backend dev)
  • Write your first technical post: one thing you learned about Spring Boot, Hibernate, or system design
  • Comment on 3–5 posts per week by people at target companies
  • Apply to 5–8 more roles. Track in a spreadsheet: company, date, JD link, status
  • Start Redis basics (free — Redis University online). Adds a high-value keyword
Month 2
Consistency + Skill Signal
  • 1 LinkedIn post per week, every week. Alternate: technical lesson / career story / opinion.
  • 10–15 applications per week. Track everything. Follow up after 1 week if no response.
  • Pin Bill-Hive v2 and Anigaze on GitHub. Add topic tags and proper descriptions.
  • Ask a Net Tech India classmate or colleague for a LinkedIn recommendation
  • Complete a short AWS or Docker basics course — adds "currently learning" credibility
Month 3
Interview-Ready + Negotiation
  • Do mock interviews: practice Java core, Spring Boot internals, REST design, DB questions, system design basics
  • Know your story cold: non-IT → Java developer, building two products, Bill-Hive, your transition
  • Research salary ranges before any interview. Know your number and be ready to name it
  • If you get an offer below ₹6L from a product company — it's negotiable. Ask once, clearly, with a reason
  • Keep posting on LinkedIn even while interviewing — visibility keeps options open
🎯 The one thing that matters most

Your story — BMS background, 120+ clients audited, crossed into tech, built two products nearly alone — is genuinely unusual. Most Java developers at 1 year have done tutorial projects and service-company work. You shipped real products. Lead with that in every cover note, every application, every LinkedIn post, every interview opening. It is your strongest differentiator.
